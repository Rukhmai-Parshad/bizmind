Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what data BizMind handles and why. BizMind is run by Rukhmai Parshad. For any question about your data, write to rukhmaiparshad@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, your password (stored only as a salted hash, never as plain text) and your role (admin, analyst or viewer).
  • Datasets: the name and type you give each dataset, the upload date, and the rows parsed from your CSV file (sales, marketing or finance records).
  • Decisions: the decisions the rules generate from your rows.
  • Sessions and tokens: a session identifier while you are logged in. If you use the API, the server keeps a record of the refresh tokens it issues so that they can be revoked.

Uploaded CSV files

The file you upload is stored only long enough to be read. It is deleted once processing has finished, and it is also deleted if processing fails. The parsed rows and the decisions stay in your account.

Who can see your data

Inside the app and the API, every dataset, row and decision is limited to the account that uploaded it. The site administrator can also view stored data through the administration interface, for support and maintenance.

Cookies

  • Session cookie: keeps you logged in.
  • CSRF cookie: protects forms from forged requests.
  • Messages cookie: may carry a short notice to the next page, for example that you have been logged out.

There are no analytics cookies, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Third parties

BizMind loads no third party scripts, fonts, analytics or advertising. The code contains nothing that shares your data with advertisers or analytics services.

Protection against password guessing

To slow down repeated login and sign up attempts, the server keeps short lived counters in memory: 15 minutes for logins and one hour for sign ups. They are keyed by a hash of your email address and your IP address.

Retention and deletion

Your account, rows and decisions are kept until you ask for them to be deleted. To request deletion, write to rukhmaiparshad@gmail.com. Deleting an account also deletes its datasets, rows and decisions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
