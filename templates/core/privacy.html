Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

BizMind is run by Rukhmai Parshad, who decides why and how your personal data is processed. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the DPDP Act) that makes Rukhmai Parshad the Data Fiduciary and you the Data Principal. This page explains what BizMind collects, why, for how long, and what you can do about it. Questions and complaints go to rukhmaiparshad@gmail.com.

Who this service is for

BizMind is for people aged 18 or over. When you sign up, you confirm that you are 18 or older. We do not check your age, and we will delete an account if we learn that it belongs to someone under 18.

Why we process your data

  • To create your account and let you log in.
  • To read the CSV file you upload and generate decisions from it.
  • To keep the service secure, for example by slowing down repeated login attempts.
  • To contact you about your account, including if a data breach affects you.

We do not use your data for marketing, advertising, profiling or selling. BizMind sends no emails by itself. If we write to you, it is a message from Rukhmai Parshad to the address on your account.

What we do not collect

BizMind does not ask for your name, phone number, address, date of birth, location or payment details, and it does not record when you last logged in. It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Personal data inside your CSV files

BizMind only needs business figures such as dates, products, regions, spend and revenue. Please leave out personal data about other people, such as customer names, phone numbers or email addresses. If a file contains such data, BizMind will store it with your rows until you delete your account.

The uploaded file itself

The file you upload is stored only long enough to be read. It is deleted once processing has finished, and it is also deleted if processing fails.

Where your data is stored

The BizMind web service and its database run on Render, in its Singapore region. This means your data is stored outside India.

Who can see your data

  • You. Inside the app and the API, every dataset, row and decision is limited to the account that uploaded it.
  • The site administrator, through the administration interface, for support and maintenance.
  • The hosting provider, which runs the servers and the database on which the data is stored.

BizMind loads no third party scripts, fonts, analytics or advertising, and the code contains nothing that shares your data with advertisers or analytics services.

Your consent

We process your account data with your consent, which you give by ticking the box when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time: delete your account in Settings, or write to rukhmaiparshad@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent means we can no longer provide the service to you, and your data is erased as described below.

Your rights

  • Access: you can see your account details, datasets and decisions in the app, and the API lists your parsed rows. For a summary of all the personal data we hold about you, write to us.
  • Correction: write to us to correct your email address or other account details. To correct data in a file, upload a corrected file as a new dataset.
  • Erasure: open Settings and choose Delete my account. It needs your password and a confirmation, deletes your account, datasets, rows and decisions at once, and logs you out. You can also ask us to do it.
  • Grievance: if you have a complaint about how your data is handled, write to rukhmaiparshad@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the DPDP Act.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account, rows and decisions until you delete your account or ask us to. Deleting your account removes them from the BizMind database straight away, together with your login sessions and API token records. If the hosting provider keeps backups, deleted data may remain in them until they expire. The short lived counters and the provider's logs described above expire on their own schedule.

If there is a data breach

If a personal data breach affects your data, we will email you at the address on your account, without delay. The message will say what happened, what data was involved, what we are doing about it and what you can do. We will also report the breach to the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

How we protect your data

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. In production the site redirects to HTTPS, marks its cookies as Secure and sends a strict transport security header. Access to every dataset is checked against the account that owns it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password that you do not use elsewhere.

Cookies

BizMind uses only the cookies it needs to work. They are listed in the Cookie Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

Contact and grievance officer
