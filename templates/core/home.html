Supported files

Works with your CSV

Sales

Needs date, product, region, units sold and unit price. Rules look at total revenue, weak regions and product concentration.

Marketing

Needs date, campaign, channel, impressions, clicks, conversions, spend and revenue. Rules look at click rate, return on ad spend and the weakest channel.

Finance

Needs date, category, department, amount and type (income or expense). Rules look at net loss, the expense ratio and the biggest expense category.

Three steps

How it works

  1. Upload a CSV

    Choose sales, marketing or finance, then pick the file. Files up to 10 MB and 50,000 rows are accepted, and the upload page lists the columns each type needs.

  2. Rules run automatically

    Fixed, documented rules check your data as soon as the upload is read. There is no model to train and nothing to configure.

  3. Read your decisions

    Each decision has a recommendation, an action, the reason it was raised and a confidence score between 0 and 1.
