Turn a CSV of business data into clear decisions.

BizMind reads your sales, marketing or finance spreadsheet, applies simple, documented rules, and tells you what to do next, with a confidence score and the reason behind each decision. It is built for small-business owners and analysts who want an answer, not another dashboard.

How it works

  1. Upload a CSV. Choose sales, marketing or finance data. Extra columns are fine.
  2. BizMind checks it against its rules. Each rule is simple and explained, such as low revenue or a weak region.
  3. Read your decisions. Every decision shows a recommendation, why it was made and how confident the rule is.

About BizMind

Most businesses collect plenty of data but still ask one question: what should I do next? BizMind puts decisions first and charts second. Your uploaded data is private to your account.
